# AI-assistance disclosure draft

Private author-review draft, 7 October 2026. The proposed statement is included in the private review article as a draft. Final author review and approval remain pending.

## Proposed article statement

OpenAI Codex assistants contributed to literature retrieval and synthesis, evidence appraisal, data-processing and evaluation code, tests, numerical checks, figures, and manuscript drafting and revision. Assistance included substantive research judgments. On 7 October 2026, the user delegated the remaining admission decisions to the assistant; seven relationships were admitted through assistant-curated judgments documented in the [dated decision record](../protocol/decisions/2026-10-07-delegated-admission-dispositions.md). These decisions were not independent human validation or evidence that the user personally reviewed every label.

Automated checks and same-host reruns tested specified computational outputs. They do not establish independent scientific replication or validate every biological interpretation. The manuscript reports their scope and limits.

Saved worker records requested `gpt-6-astra` with `medium` reasoning for manuscript, figure and admission work, but actual serving receipts were unavailable. These requested settings do not verify the model that served each task. A practical record of tools, available model/version information, dates and uses will be supplied at final author review, retaining unresolved model provenance explicitly. AI systems are not authors; responsibility for the submitted work rests with its human authors.
