## Experimental figure and processing appraisal

The next pass visually inspected the retained supporting figure on PDF page 201. Panel c shows the reported wild-type phoenicin signal and its loss in the perturbed sample. This supports the paper's experimental assignment; it does not demonstrate complete pathway sufficiency, exclude every alternative explanation, or validate feature 38 in the separate metabolomics export. The figure image is retained as `reference-figure.png`; future work should reuse it.

The metabolomics methods on PDF page 158 establish that the in-house PCDL annotation used MassHunter feature picking, while the GNPS feature pool used MZmine 2.53. Both operated on the study's long-method acquisitions, but their identifiers are not automatically interchangeable. Thus the unique rounded-mass match remains an inferred crosswalk across processing outputs.
