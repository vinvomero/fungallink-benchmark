Subject: Existing FAC expression feature tables for a fungal benchmark

Dear Professor Kelleher,

I'm an independent researcher developing FungalLink, a comparative benchmark for fungal genome-to-metabolite linking tools. We have retained the published FAC-MS supplements, available sequence records and public metabolomics deposits, but have not found the complete processed feature tables for the FAC expression experiments.

Could you point us to an existing deposit or export containing the full feature tables, the FAC-construct/sample-column map and any mapping from published targets to feature rows? We need the measured candidate features, with m/z, retention time and sample intensities, to evaluate retrieval of supported products.

Our cases include benzomalvin A/D, facms0005 and valactamides A–H from the 2017 study (10.1038/nchembio.2408), acu-dioxomorpholine A and terreazepine from the later FAC studies, and three measured intermediates from the 2018 benzomalvin follow-up (10.1021/acs.biochem.8b00076): the linear tripeptide, macrocycle-associated compound 2 and desmethyl-associated compound 4. We retain the proposed chemical identities and their uncertainties.

For context, MSV000081097 contains species-labelled donor acquisitions, and MSV000082097/MSV000082100 provide identical selected-spectrum files. We have not treated these as complete FAC expression-screen matrices.

A link or existing files would be enough; no new experiments or reprocessing are requested. If the full tables or mappings were not retained, knowing that would help us close the acquisition question. Please indicate any attribution and reuse terms for files supplied outside public deposits.

Thank you,
