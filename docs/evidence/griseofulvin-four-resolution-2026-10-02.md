## Structure comparison

[Cacho et al. (2013), DOI 10.1021/cb400541z](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/29769/cdc_29769_DS1.pdf), retained author manuscript, Scheme 1 on PDF page 13, draws compounds 13 and 15 with the free aromatic OH on the carbon directly adjacent to the carbonyl-side fused carbon. The other aromatic oxygen substituent is methoxy. Caesar Supplementary Figure 1, PDF page 21 (printed page 20), first and second panels, instead draws the 6-O products with methoxy at that carbon and OH at the other aromatic position. Both figures were inspected directly in this follow-up. Rotating the drawings does not remove this difference: the carbonyl-side fused carbon supplies the same structural landmark in both.

Cacho Figure 1A, PDF page 16, uses a biosynthetic carbon-numbering scheme; the paragraph spanning pages 4–5 calls the unmethylated site in compounds 13–15 position 5. That number must not be copied directly into conventional product nomenclature. The ruling here is limited to the different drawn connectivity, without assigning a replacement canonical structure identifier. The earlier concern is closed as an unsupported equivalence of the published drawings, not a judgment that Caesar's measured features were misidentified.
