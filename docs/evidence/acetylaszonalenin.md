[Yin et al. 2009](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M807606200) established the AnaPT prenylation and AnaAT acetylation steps by biochemical experiments and product characterization. Its AnaPS assignment was proposed, not a complete native-locus deletion result. This is partial experimental pathway support, which must remain visible when choosing the evidence class. The current review read the primary indexed records; full-page requests to ScienceDirect and the latter PMC page were blocked. The earlier local packet's broader full-text inspection is historical evidence, not a new full-text review.
