## The scope decision

The contract requires competing-locus checks for reference transfer. That requirement should answer the ambiguity of the proposed query. An experimentally implicated reference-containing family does not claim that every family member produces the product, or that no other locus contributes. Likewise, finding another producing locus would not automatically invalidate a supported positive: the benchmark permits multiple supported relationships.

For the two decisions below, the bounded alternative-locus assessment checks whether the sampled source region is the reference annotation rather than a different same-chromosome locus, whether alternate family labels duplicate that locus, and whether the primary experiment establishes a product-specific association. It does not claim an assembly-wide absence search. Accepting that assessment for the stated assembly-defined scope requires a recorded researcher criterion before applying it across cases. A different historical culture's exact causal BGC, all-member production, or exclusivity would need more evidence. No outside review or new experiment is imposed here.

That is a limit on pathway completeness, not a report that another autonomous locus is the demonstrated producer. This inspection used indexed primary full text; direct PMC opening returned a browser challenge.

The eleven-member TERPENE_40 roster includes members outside the twelve-member NRPKS_38 roster; no all-member product claim follows from their names.

Recommend reference-supported `source:TERPENE_40 -> feature3485`, anchored specifically to the FMGX0049 assembly-defined reference locus, with the existing eleven-member roster retained. Do not call the assayed wild-type culture directly perturbed. Decision: accept the ordered record identity and reference-background correspondence as sufficient for this reference-supported family association, with the bounded ambiguity assessment above and no exclusivity claim? A single-locus retrieval alternative must be declared as a new query rather than silently replacing TERPENE_40.
