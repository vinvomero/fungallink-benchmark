# Source attribution and numerical release basis

This notice accompanies the three numerical reconstruction candidates. Original contributions use the author-approved licences in `licenses/SCOPE.md`. It does not apply a blanket licence to third-party source material or authorize posting the private review archive.

## Caesar numerical records

Cite Caesar LK, Butun FA, Robey MT and colleagues, Correlative metabologenomics of110 fungi reveals metabolite–gene cluster pairs, Nature Chemical Biology19,846–854(2023), doi:10.1038/s41589-023-01276-8. Public study identifiers include BioProject PRJNA852164 and MassIVE MSV000089848. The publisher-hosted processed feature workbook and selected GCF archive remain separately identified source inputs. Their byte hashes and download locators are preserved in existing source records.

The release candidates use project-generated numerical representations and identifiers rather than copies of the publisher workbook layout, GCF HTML archive, articles or author-supplied workbook. Six core inputs/gold objects were inspected recursively: all string values are identifiers of at most35 characters; the other leaves are numerical values. Project scripts transformed the measurements into frozen presence vectors and query membership, generated the rankings and native-fit logs, and evaluated the supported target sets. These factual vectors remain derived from the source study; transformation does not create new biological evidence or erase prior exposure.

The proposed release basis is reuse of factual numerical data with attribution and licensing of the project's original code, curation and generated expression. The U.S. Copyright Office distinguishes facts from original compilation authorship; this distinction supports treating factual numerical inputs separately from copied source publications. It is not a conclusion that every source compilation, contract or jurisdiction has been adjudicated. No new permission request is assumed necessary merely because a CC notice was not located. [Copyright Office facts guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/what-is-copyright/) and [database scope](https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-databases.html).

The historical label-to-strain bridge used author-supplied information. Lindsay K. Caesar supplied the historical strain-label workbook and clarified labels. Vin's sample maps are project-generated reconciliations incorporating those clarifications and marked inferences; matching retained attachments establish their origin. No claim of wholly public mapping reconstruction is made. The raw author workbook, correspondence and publisher reading copies are excluded from the numerical candidates. The author reviews this exact derived-data scope at the final release gate.

This versioned decision narrows the earlier broad source-dependent package hold on the inspected numerical candidates. It does not overwrite historical rights inventories or frozen inputs, nor does it clear raw source copies for redistribution.
