## Finite release rules

1. Include original source code, synthetic fixtures, schema definitions and build/evaluation recipes only after owner/contributor approval of their license. Inspect fixtures for real-data copies before selecting them. Preserve tool versions and dependency manifests, but install dependencies rather than copying environments.
2. Include original factual curation, citations, admissible result tables and provenance manifests after removing private correspondence, copied source-row payloads, source excerpts and local machine paths. Hold measurement arrays and mapping derivatives until their named dependencies above are cleared. An accession-only manifest can be released without bundling the underlying workbook.
3. For primary deposits whose licenses cover the exact selected files, retain record title, creator attribution where required, accession/version, original filename, source URL, checksum, license URL and transformation description. This is conditional inclusion under source terms, not a blanket project license.
4. Exclude publisher and author manuscript PDFs, cached article XML/HTML, supplementary literature supplied only for reading, correspondence and contact details, local import configuration, machine-specific rerun locations, scratch directories, software installations and unrelated cohort-screen payloads. Keep citations and original factual summaries. Exclude unauthenticated supplied maps until permission or public reconstruction resolves them.

The remaining dependencies are finite: resolve the two publisher supplement payloads; resolve both kinds of supplied mapping and their copies; approve licenses for original contributions; then construct and inspect a new public allowlist package. A download recipe can address the publisher files, but it cannot replace a mapping that has no authenticated public source. The sample tables are supplied factual tabulations; this review does not establish that they are private correspondence or determine ownership of individual facts. The historical member crosswalk has a documented author-workbook origin, but no attached redistribution statement. These are release-provenance gaps, not a finding that the scientific results cannot be published. Public release remains a separate user-authorized action. No permission request, correspondence, deposition, publication or visibility change occurred, and this review gives no legal guarantee.
