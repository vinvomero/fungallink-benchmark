## Extend the analysis by representation

**Genomic comparison, about one to two working days.** Reuse the saved exact-match and reference-correspondence results when their sequence hashes and metric definitions match. For new comparisons, extract only accepted locus proteins and proteins in the seven source-selected GCF rosters; retain member IDs and provenance. Report completeness by locus and family. A GCF is a set of loci: give within-family and between-family distributions over represented members, their coverage and extremes. A single anchor represents only that locus. If member proteins are missing, do not claim family-wide similarity.

Compare annotated core enzymes of compatible biosynthetic roles using the existing Biopython global alignment convention: BLOSUM62, gap open -10, extension -0.5, including terminal gaps; freeze the full aligner settings and deterministic tie handling. Record identical aligned nongap residues / paired nongap residues, paired residues / each full sequence length, score, lengths, ties and both sequence hashes. Report exact full-length duplicates separately. Within a bounded locus pair, retain reciprocal-best ties and gene order; summarize the fraction of annotated proteins with a reciprocal qualifying match under the prespecified identity/coverage rule. Use 70% and 90% identity, each with at least 80% coverage of both proteins, as descriptive screens. They do not alone establish orthology, BGC equivalence or the same product. Global alignments between incompatible enzyme classes are `not_comparable`; partial/core-only records cannot support whole-cluster dissimilarity. Homology interpretation uses the existing source-supported function, content and organization evidence.

**Spectral comparison, about one working day.** Use all saved native-joined spectra from the two packages, keeping their feature joins and acquisition provenance. This is redundancy analysis within the measured pools, not another class predictor or candidate ranking. Compare within each package; add cross-package pairs only when polarity and charge are known and compatible. Missing polarity/charge excludes that cross-package comparison with a reason. Retain source collision-energy/instrument metadata, including unknown values; stratify interpretations where settings differ rather than treating low similarity as distinct chemistry.

Freeze a primary peak rule: keep finite positive peaks at m/z >= 10, remove peaks within +/- 1 Da of the precursor when precursor m/z is known, retain peaks at least 1% of the spectrum's base peak, square-root intensities, and L2-normalize. Record before/after peak counts and unavailable/empty spectra. Use matchms CosineHungarian with tolerance 0.02 Da, mz_power=0, intensity_power=1 on those transformed intensities. Retain score and matched-peak count for every eligible pair. Flag resemblance at cosine >= 0.7 with >= 6 matched peaks; report 0.9 as the prespecified stricter sensitivity. These thresholds are descriptive choices. Exact native scan or canonical peak-list duplication is recorded separately. Do not call high cosine an isomer/adduct identity. Adduct/isotope/in-source-fragment hypotheses need native precursor, charge, retention and source evidence, with uncertain edges remaining hypotheses. No mass/RT-only bridge to the historical Caesar features is allowed.

The RDKit [fingerprint documentation](https://www.rdkit.org/docs/GettingStartedInPython.html), Biopython [alignment documentation](https://biopython.org/docs/latest/Tutorial/chapter_pairwise.html) and matchms [CosineHungarian API](https://matchms.readthedocs.io/en/latest/api/matchms.similarity.CosineHungarian.html) were consulted on 11 October 2026 for API semantics. The thresholds and preprocessing above are this analysis's declared choices, not claims of universal biological cutoffs.

## Dependence, exposure and implications for evaluation

Build typed edges, not one opaque similarity score. Separate exact query/sequence identity, curated shared pathway, shared construct/host, repeated biological sample, technical acquisition/ion duplication, chemical resemblance and spectral resemblance. Every edge carries endpoint namespace, source, confidence/status and rule version. Study/candidate-pool sharing is an analysis-design dependence, not automatically chemical identity. Missing comparisons cannot certify independence.

Keep the original analysis groups unchanged. Create separately versioned proposed groups by connected components of established dependence edges relevant to the stated resampling or split purpose, with the chosen edge types printed. Add conservative sensitivity components containing unresolved plausible-dependence edges and prespecified similarity flags; show which edges cause mergers. These are grouping scenarios, not newly discovered independent replicates. Any proposal to split the current conservative complete-analysis group requires positive scientific justification and a separate protocol decision; absence of a computed edge is insufficient.

For each scenario, count relationships, unique queries, represented pathway groups and complete-evaluation groups separately. Assess whether a future grouped holdout could retain usable queries, positives, input eligibility and candidate pools on both sides while keeping shared entities together. If dependencies connect the population or unresolved exposure prevents the desired claim, report that the holdout is not established. Do not create random-row folds, manufacture a minimum group count, or relabel the retrospective benchmark as unseen-pathway generalization.

Bound exposure work to methods already represented in the task-exposure table and their actually consumed assets. Separate (1) known source/label selection and historical rank exposure, (2) exact public-reference membership, (3) verifiable model-training membership and (4) sequence/chemical/spectral similarity to a declared accessible reference set. Check stable versioned BGC/library IDs, exact structure identifiers and sequence/spectrum hashes where the relevant full records are retained. A reference-table match is not proof of training membership. The bundled class table, Rosetta mappings or a Spec2Class example CSV cannot substitute for a complete training roster. Do not search every model database or download new model weights.

Exposure statuses are `confirmed_overlap`, `no_match_in_declared_complete_roster`, `partial_roster_no_match`, `unknown`, and `not_applicable`; store searched universe, asset hash, matching rule and coverage. Never shorten the two no-match statuses to “unexposed.” Deterministic controls have no learned training set, but remain exposed to retrospective source selection and method development. Class-predictor training exposure stays unknown wherever the weight-specific roster cannot be verified. Annotate each finding's method layer and its relationship coverage in a new companion table; retain `task-exposure-v1.tsv` unchanged.

Use the results to qualify existing benchmark interpretation and design later analyses. No retrieval scores or performance aggregates are recomputed in this task. Any later group-weighted performance comparison requires its own prespecified analytical version, preserving `protocol/analysis-plan.md`, matched populations and full coverage denominators. With one defensible complete-analysis group, between-group uncertainty is not estimable. Unknown links remain unlabeled; similarity is not validation of a genome-product relationship.
